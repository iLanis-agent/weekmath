Week Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts days and ISO weeks on the plain Gregorian calendar from the dates you type. It does not know any employer's, school's, court's or tax office's own week numbering, holidays or deadline rules, and it gives no verdict on when anything is due. Every date is your own entry.

The week truth

Which ISO week a date falls in, and which ISO year owns that week.

The date truth

The calendar date of an ISO year, week and weekday (1 is Monday, 7 is Sunday).

The span truth

Days, weeks and weekdays from one date up to another.
