Week Math

Monday 30 December 2024 is already in week 1 of 2025, and 2026 has a week 53.

Boundary: arithmetic only Week Math counts days and ISO weeks on the plain Gregorian calendar from the dates you type. It does not know any employer's, school's, court's or tax office's own week numbering, holidays or deadline rules, and it gives no verdict on when anything is due. Every date is your own entry.

Three questions, each answered exactly. Here are the starting examples.

The week truth

2024-12-30 is a Monday. It falls in ISO week 1 of 2025, Monday 2024-12-30 to Sunday 2025-01-05. ISO year 2025 has 52 weeks. The ISO year 2025 differs from the calendar year 2024.

The date truth

ISO year 2026, week 53, weekday 4 is Thursday 2026-12-31. ISO year 2026 has 53 weeks.

The span truth

From 2026-01-01 to 2026-03-01 is 59 days (8 weeks and 3 days). Counting the earlier date but not the later one: 42 weekdays (Monday to Friday) and 17 weekend days.
